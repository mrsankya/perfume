## Architecture & Key Features
1. **Hero Carousel**: Responsive viewport slider with high-resolution luxury editorial photography, automated auto-play timer (4s), touch gesture swipe support, side navigation cursors, and smooth fade transitions.
2. **Dynamic Fragrance Catalog**: Multi-attribute filtering (Accords: Floral, Royal Oud, Warm Vanilla, Smoky Woods, Fresh Citrus; Gender: All, Women, Men, Unisex; Real-time search query).
3. **Interactive Scent Quiz**: 3-step recommendation engine (Occasion, Fragrance Vibe, Gender Preference) with instant product match and direct WhatsApp reservation CTA.
4. **Instant WhatsApp Reservation Engine**: Pre-filled reservation links for physical boutique store pickup with price, perfume name, and brand.
5. **Mobile Navigation Dock**: Fixed blur-backdrop bottom app dock with active scrollspy section tracking.
6. **Quick View Modal**: Detailed fragrance notes, accords, batch inspection details, and reservation actions.
